# MW-BAAS — Brand & Product Narrative

## Brand essence

MW-BAAS is the shared backend foundation that helps MoonWitness applications operate as one coherent, accountable ecosystem—without surrendering their individual identity.

**Primary tagline:** The Infrastructure Behind Every Witness.

**Signature line:** One Foundation. Many Applications. Accountable by Design.

## The narrative

The MoonWitness vision begins with a responsibility: the world should be able to inspect what is recorded, understand where claims came from, and distinguish original evidence from interpretation, testimony, hypothesis, and creative work.

That responsibility cannot rest on words alone. It needs dependable infrastructure.

MW-BAAS is the backend foundation built for that responsibility. It gives MoonWitness products a common platform for APIs, identity, access control, storage, background processing, notifications, integrations, and operational services. Each application can focus on its own purpose while relying on shared engineering foundations.

MW-BAAS is not an oracle, a source of ultimate truth, or an authority over human judgment. It is infrastructure: it helps systems preserve records, apply declared policies, expose provenance, and make operations inspectable. The meaning and consequences of information remain matters for responsible people and institutions.

At its data foundation, MW-BAAS is designed to integrate with MoonWitness ORM (MW-ORM), an independently evolving relational engine and modular data platform. MW-ORM provides reusable data infrastructure; MW-BAAS composes backend services and application-facing capabilities. Neither project should absorb the other's responsibility.

This is how applications can grow without becoming disconnected systems—or one monolith that cannot evolve.

## Brand architecture

- **Empire of the Light** — parent vision and ecosystem context.
- **MoonWitness** — public-facing knowledge, witness, research, and storytelling ecosystem.
- **MW-BAAS** — shared backend platform and application-facing infrastructure.
- **MW-ORM** — independent relational and modular data foundation.
- **Applications** — focused products built on explicit contracts.

ROCKSOUL remains a separate identity and should not be folded into MW-BAAS branding without an explicit product decision.

## Promise

1. **Consistency** — shared infrastructure and predictable contracts.
2. **Traceability** — records and operations that can be inspected and audited where required.
3. **Composability** — capabilities assembled as modules rather than copied into every application.
4. **Security and privacy** — clear identity, access, consent, and data boundaries.
5. **Operational reliability** — observable services, controlled migrations, diagnosable failures.
6. **Developer clarity** — documented APIs, versioned contracts, reproducible integration.

These are product commitments and engineering goals. Each must be backed by implementation and tests before being presented as a shipped capability.

## Voice and personality

- Precise, not grandiose: prefer evidence and explicit scope over limitless claims.
- Confident, not absolute: state what works and what remains planned.
- Human-centered, not anthropomorphic: the platform supports human work.
- Modular, not fragmented: shared contracts enable independent products.
- Transparent, not performative: explain provenance, limitations, and status.

## Message pillars

### A common foundation
A consistent backend reduces duplicated infrastructure and makes shared capabilities easier to maintain.

### Data with clear ownership
MW-ORM is the reusable relational foundation. MW-BAAS is the backend service platform. Applications own their domain behavior. Each application has one schema and migration authority.

### Evidence remains inspectable
MoonWitness products may handle records, testimony, research, and interpretation. The platform should preserve provenance, consent, access history, and review boundaries where required.

### Humans remain accountable
Automation can support discovery, organization, and operations. It does not turn uncertain claims into facts or transfer responsibility away from people.

## Short descriptions

**One sentence:** MW-BAAS is the modular backend platform for MoonWitness applications, designed to connect shared services with the MW-ORM data foundation through explicit, accountable contracts.

**Short paragraph:** MW-BAAS gives MoonWitness applications a shared backend for APIs, identity, access, storage, jobs, notifications, and integrations. Built to consume MW-ORM as an independent data foundation, it supports a modular ecosystem while keeping product logic, data ownership, and human accountability clear.

**Elevator pitch:** MoonWitness is building an ecosystem where records and claims remain inspectable. MW-BAAS supplies shared backend infrastructure that helps its applications work together responsibly. MW-ORM provides the independent relational foundation; BAAS composes services; each application remains in control of its domain.

## Visual direction

- **Mood:** quiet confidence, precision, clarity, stewardship, technical depth.
- **Core motif:** a lunar horizon or precise orbital arc paired with a structured record/grid motif.
- **Avoid:** generic SaaS rocket imagery, mystical omniscience claims, surveillance aesthetics, or imagery implying the platform determines truth.
- **Color direction:** deep midnight/navy, restrained lunar silver, warm ivory, and a small signal accent. Maintain accessible contrast.
- **Typography:** highly legible modern sans-serif for product UI; restrained display typography for editorial moments.
- **Logo behavior:** retain MoonWitness family resemblance while giving MW-BAAS a distinct infrastructure mark, legible in monochrome, favicon, CLI, and documentation.

## Product naming and capability language

Use **MW-BAAS** in engineering contexts and **MoonWitness Backend as a Service** as its expanded descriptive name. Use **The Infrastructure Behind Every Witness** as the primary public tagline.

Do not describe MW-BAAS as an ORM, ERP, truth engine, or replacement for human review.

Use:
- “designed to integrate with MW-ORM” until adapter compatibility is proven;
- “planned module runtime” until the kernel is implemented and tested;
- “tenant isolation controls” only after security review and bypass-path testing;
- “audit-ready” only for flows with defined event coverage, retention, and access controls.

Avoid “fully integrated,” “production-ready,” “tamper-proof,” and “absolute truth” unless the relevant claims are demonstrably true.
